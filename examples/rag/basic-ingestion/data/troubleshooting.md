# VerdaPod — Troubleshooting

If the ring light blinks red, the reservoir is empty: refill it up to the MAX
line. Three short blue pulses mean the pod lost its Bluetooth pairing —
re-pair it from the app. If the pump makes a rattling noise, remove the
reservoir, rinse the intake filter under warm water, and reseat it. A pod
that stops responding entirely can be reset by holding the leaf button for
fifteen seconds; the ring light flashes white twice when the reset completes.
Resetting does not erase your plant history, which is stored in the app.
