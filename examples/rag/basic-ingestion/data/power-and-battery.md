# VerdaPod — Power and battery

The VerdaPod runs on an internal rechargeable battery. A full charge keeps the
pod running for about ten days with the grow light on its default schedule.
Charging uses the USB-C cable supplied in the box and takes roughly three
hours from empty. While charging, the ring light breathes amber; it turns
solid white when the battery is full. Battery health is shown in the app
under Settings, and a low-battery notification is sent at fifteen percent.
