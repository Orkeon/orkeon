# VerdaPod — Warranty and returns

Every VerdaPod is covered by a two-year limited warranty starting on the date
of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in the pump, the grow
light, and the battery, but not damage caused by drops, immersion, or
third-party seed capsules. Unopened pods can be returned within thirty days
for a full refund; opened pods within fourteen days for store credit. To open
a warranty claim, use the Support tab in the app and keep your order number
at hand.
