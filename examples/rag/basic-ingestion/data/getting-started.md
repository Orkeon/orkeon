# VerdaPod — Getting started

The VerdaPod is a self-watering indoor herb garden. Unbox the pod, fill the
reservoir up to the MAX line with tap water, and drop one seed capsule into
each of the three slots. Download the VerdaPod companion app, create an
account, and pair the pod over Bluetooth by holding the leaf button for five
seconds until the ring light pulses green. The first sprouts usually appear
after four to seven days depending on the capsule variety.
