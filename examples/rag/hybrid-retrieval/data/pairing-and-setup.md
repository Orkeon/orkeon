# AtlasCam — Pairing and setup

Mount the camera, power it with the supplied adapter, and open the AtlasCam
app. Tap "Add device", scan the QR code under the base, and join the 2.4 GHz
Wi-Fi network. Pairing takes about a minute; the LED turns solid green when
the camera is online. If pairing stalls, move the camera closer to the router
during the first setup.
