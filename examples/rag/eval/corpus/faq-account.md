# Nimbus Hub FAQ — Account and sign-in

Your Nimbus account manages every hub and sensor paired to your home.

To reset a lost password, open the companion app, tap "Forgot password" on the
sign-in screen, and enter the email address of your account. We send a six-digit
verification code valid for 15 minutes; entering it lets you choose a new
password immediately.

Accounts are locked for one hour after ten failed sign-in attempts. Two-factor
authentication can be enabled from the Security tab of the account settings.
