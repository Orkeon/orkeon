# Nimbus Hub FAQ — Nimbus Plus subscription

Nimbus Plus adds cloud video history, extended automation rules, and priority
support to any Nimbus Hub. Plans are billed monthly or yearly.

You can cancel Nimbus Plus at any time from the Subscription tab of the
companion app. Yearly plans cancelled mid-term are refunded on a prorated
basis: you pay only for the full months already used, and the remainder is
returned to your payment method.

Downgrading to the free tier keeps all local automations working; only the
cloud features stop at the end of the paid period.
