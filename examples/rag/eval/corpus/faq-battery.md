# Nimbus Sense FAQ — Battery life and replacement

How long does the Nimbus Sense battery last? A fresh battery in a Nimbus Sense
lasts about six months with default settings. The battery is a standard CR123A
cell; the battery is not rechargeable, so you cannot make the battery last
longer by charging it — when the battery runs out, replace it.

How do I know the battery is low? The companion app shows the battery level of
every Nimbus Sense, and the hub chimes when a battery drops below 15%.

Where do I buy a new battery? Replacement battery two-packs for the Nimbus
Sense are sold in the accessory store. Batteries carry a 90-day warranty
against manufacturing defects.
