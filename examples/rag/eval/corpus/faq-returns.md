# Nimbus Hub FAQ — Returns and refunds

Customers may return a Nimbus Hub for a full refund within 30 days of delivery.
The device must come back in its original packaging with all accessories.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the
return being received at our warehouse.

Opened spare battery packs for the Nimbus Sense are non-refundable: once the
battery seal is broken we cannot restock the battery for safety reasons. Unused
battery packs in sealed packaging follow the normal 30-day window.

A 10% restocking fee applies to returns without the original packaging.
