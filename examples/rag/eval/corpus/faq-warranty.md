# Nimbus Hub FAQ — Warranty

The Nimbus Hub and Nimbus Sense carry a two-year limited hardware warranty
covering manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover the Nimbus Sense battery wearing out: a battery
that lasts less time than expected is considered normal wear, because how long
a battery lasts depends on placement, temperature, and polling settings. A
battery that is dead on arrival is exchanged free of charge within 14 days.

Warranty service is requested from the companion app; a prepaid return label
is emailed within one business day.
