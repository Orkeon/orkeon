# Nimbus Hub FAQ — Shipping

Standard shipping delivers the Nimbus Hub within 5 business days in mainland
Europe and North America. Express shipping (2 business days) is available at
checkout for an additional fee.

Replacement battery packs for the Nimbus Sense ship separately by ground
freight, because lithium battery regulations forbid air transport of loose
batteries. A battery shipment can therefore take up to 10 business days even
when the rest of the order arrives sooner.

We do not ship to freight forwarders or post-office boxes.
