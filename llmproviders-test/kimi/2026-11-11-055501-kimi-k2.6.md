A Kimi verdict is not reproducible in the same sense as a verdict at temperature 0.
- Two regional endpoints with **separate accounts**: `api.moonshot.ai` (international, default) and `api.moonshot.cn` (mainland China).
- G-11 closed: check that the host resolved in the report header is the expected one.
- The `moonshot-v1-*` series counts input + output within the same bound — its “8 K” is not an 8 K input window.
- 2026-10-07, two acts. At 18:09 the account was suspended (`exceeded_current_quota_error`, HTTP 429, `suspended due to insufficient balance`): the retry policy treats that 429 as a rate limit and spends its whole budget on every mode, so the run was stopped after nine minutes and nothing was archived — the refusal quotes the organisation and key ids. - `kimi-k2.7-code` mandates temperature 1 like its siblings, and the provider's own remedy hides it on half the paths (measured 2026-10-07): on a buffered call `KimiLlmProvider` reads `invalid temperature: only 1 is allowed for this model`, retries once with the mandated value and logs it — so M1, M5 and M7 pass at temperature 0 — while the two streaming paths have no such retry and surface the refusal (M3 and M4 red, 9/2/1 at 19:17, kept as the trace).
