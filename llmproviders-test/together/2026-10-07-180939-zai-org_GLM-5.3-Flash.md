## Points of attention (matrix §6.6)

- The default `Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo` is text-only: its red M9 is the D-03 proof (2026-08-30 campaign). No vision companion was declarable until 2026-09-21: the 4 vision models tested on 2026-08-30 (`Qwen3-VL-8B`, `Qwen2.5-VL-72B`, `Llama-4-Scout`, `Llama-4-Maverick`) were all non-serverless on the measured tier — paid dedicated endpoint required, failure report archived as proof. `zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash`, the replacement candidate, reads the image in serverless (M9 ✅ twice on 2026-09-21) and is the companion since.
- LLM-10 (2026-09-19) sent the context window as `max_tokens` here, betting on `context_length_exceeded_behavior: truncate`; the 2026-09-21 campaign measured the default at 2/9/1 (`inputs tokens + max_new_tokens must be <= 131073`) — two of three serverless engines refuse regardless of the flag, the third clamps on the buffered path only. The window entries left the catalogue the same morning (`fb00251a`), the 4096 fallback is back (the field omitted means 2048 at Together, measured), and the 2/9/1 report is kept as the trace.
- Endpoint and default model up to date. One M1–M5 pass is enough.
- Together serves DeepSeek-V4-Pro at 512 K where DeepSeek serves it at 1 M — a service gap to verify in M11.
