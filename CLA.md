## Why this exists

Orkeon is published under the MIT licence and is intended to be transferred, in time,
to a foundation that will hold it on behalf of its users. That transfer — and any later
change of licence a foundation might decide — is only possible if every contribution
came with clear rights. Asking for them at the first external contribution costs one
file; asking for them after a hundred contributions means finding a hundred people.

This agreement does **not** take your copyright. You keep it, and you may do anything
else you like with your contribution. It gives the project a licence broad enough to
distribute, relicense and transfer your contribution together with the rest of Orkeon.

## Definitions

- **"You"** — the individual accepting this agreement, and the legal entity on whose
  behalf you contribute if your contribution is made in the course of your employment
  and your employer holds rights in it.
- **"Contribution"** — any original work of authorship (code, tests, documentation,
  configuration, designs) that you intentionally submit to the Orkeon project for
  inclusion, by pull request or any other means, excluding anything you clearly mark
  as "not a contribution".
- **"Project Steward"** — the current maintainer of the Orkeon project, **and any
  successor entity** to which the maintainer transfers the project's stewardship (for
  example a foundation). Rights granted below to the Project Steward pass to the
  successor without any further action from you.

## How to accept

Acceptance is recorded per GitHub account, once. On your first pull request a check
posts a comment; reply on that pull request with the exact sentence it asks for. The
signature is stored on the `cla-signatures` branch of this repository, in the open, and
covers every later contribution from the same account. No email, no form, no personal
information beyond the GitHub account that already authored the pull request.

To withdraw a signature, open an issue saying so; contributions already accepted remain
licensed as above, since the project has distributed them in reliance on this agreement.
